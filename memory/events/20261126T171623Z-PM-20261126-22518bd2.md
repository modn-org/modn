Known limits after Milestone 15, recorded from the end-of-run review of head 37ce5b1. Each limit is advisory and not fixed.

1. `undo_activation` removes the intent before it restores the pointers. A death in between leaves a partly switched set that nothing repairs.
2. `ModuleRegistry::open` has no caller outside tests, and callers of `repair` discard its result. Users never see a rolled-back or unreadable-intent outcome.
3. ZIP sync still publishes the registration before it commits modules. Its behavior is unchanged and documented.
4. docs/buckets.md is over 500 lines (539). The AGENTS.md rule covers source files.
