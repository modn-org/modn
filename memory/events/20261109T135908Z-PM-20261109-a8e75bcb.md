Decided 2026-11-09 by the Milestone 5 orchestrator run (merged in f07494a). 'modn module update <game>' fetches the bucket that supplied the active release, the same fetch as 'bucket sync', and reports game_id, from, to and outcome. A pinned module reports outcome pinned and keeps its release. A module registered from a local folder fails with a hint to register the new version. 'modn bucket trust-modules <bucket>' without --yes prints the signer key ID, writes nothing and exits non-zero; --fingerprint binds the acknowledgement to that key. This follows the existing --yes convention in docs/cli.md.
