Decided 2026-11-07: three milestones let a mod outlive any single host.

1. Durable distribution (milestone 8, #117-#124): BLAKE3 pins, multiple sources with fallback, HTTPS catalog ZIPs, mirrors and signed relocation, author signing tools and key succession. No peer-to-peer transfer. It comes after Sources and trust and before Module ecosystem.
2. Key-addressed catalogs (milestone 9, #125-#127): users follow a signer key through pkarr records on the Mainline DHT. It comes after Modpacks and sharing, which adds iroh.
3. Peer seeding (milestone 10, #128-#133): users seed archives over iroh, found by BLAKE3 pin on the Mainline DHT.

Peer seeding ships, but it is opt-in and off by default. Modn seeds only archives whose record sets redistributable: true. A policy record (#128) on privacy and the process model must come before code. Deferred and not filed: anonymous seeding (Veilid or Tor), IPFS as an origin, peer exchange.
