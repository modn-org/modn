The theme checker accepts only values that keep a themed element in its own box. `position` takes `static` or `relative`; `z-index` takes `auto`, `0` or a negative integer; offsets (`top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`, `inset`, `inset-*`) and `margin`/`margin-*` take zero or positive literal lengths. A negative length or any function (`calc()`, `min()`, `env()`) is `forbidden-overlay`; `var()` in those properties is `forbidden-var`. The rule id `forbidden-position-fixed` stays for compatibility but now covers every position except `static` and `relative`. `.modn-main`, `.modn-view`, `.modn-details` and `.modn-library` joined `PROTECTED_CONTEXT_CLASSES`, because they are ancestors of the install review and of both approval hosts; they may change only colours and fonts. Consequence: the official Night City theme lost its `.modn-main` padding and page tile, which moved to `.modn-library__grid`. Known and documented limit: the checker cannot read what a decoration shows, so `background-image`, `box-shadow` and `outline` can still paint a fake prompt; it does stop one element from being placed over another.
