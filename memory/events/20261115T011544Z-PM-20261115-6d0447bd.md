On 2026-11-14 the remove-from-Modn confirmation (a TaskDialogIndirect box) stayed open in about one of six verifier runs because the UI Automation Invoke on its Yes button was dropped without an error. The verifier now posts TDM_CLICK_BUTTON to the dialog and fails unless the dialog window disappears. Native Yes and No buttons are localized (Sim and Nao on a pt-BR Windows), so the verifier maps the Win32 control ids 6 and 7 instead of button names.
