Implemented 2026-11-14 at 0b5f410. rotation.json plus rotation.sig is signed by the pinned key and names the successor; revocation.json plus revocation.sig is signed by the offline revocation key pinned at first sync from revocation.pub and names the replacement. Each signature covers a domain-separated statement, so one cannot replay as the other. A rotation is accepted only when the same revision publishes catalog.pub and catalog.sig for the successor. Module trust follows a rotation and is cleared by a revocation. The pinned revocation key cannot be changed in place. The author commands are modn catalog rotate and revoke.
