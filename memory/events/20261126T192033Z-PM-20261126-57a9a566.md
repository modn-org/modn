Milestone 15 limits after the undo-intent fix in PR #218. Limit 1 is fixed; limits 2 to 4 were true at 0e395cb and remain.

1. Fixed at 2fa39c4: `undo_activation` restores every `current` pointer, every moved release and every created entry first, and removes `activation.json` only when no step failed. A failed undo keeps the intent, so the next `ModuleRegistry::open` repairs the partly switched set. `repair_locked` also keeps the intent when a rollback step fails, and the next open retries. Evidence: the lib tests `death_in_the_middle_of_an_undo_keeps_the_intent_and_the_next_open_repairs_the_set` and `an_incomplete_rollback_keeps_the_intent_for_the_next_open` in `crates/modn-core/src/game_modules/registry/tests.rs`. The first failed at commit 2fa39c4's parent with `the intent stays on disk until every undo step succeeds`, and again with `assertion failed: matches!(opened.repair, Some(crate::game_modules::ActivationRepair::Completed { .. }))` when that assertion was disabled.

2. Still true: `ModuleRegistry::open` has no caller outside tests, and callers of `repair` discard its result. Users never see a rolled-back or unreadable-intent outcome.

3. Still true: ZIP sync publishes the registration before it commits modules. Its behavior is unchanged and documented.

4. Still true: docs/buckets.md is over 500 lines (539). The AGENTS.md rule covers source files.
