As of bf3a78d (2026-11-26), both the CLI and desktop halves of the THEME_INVALID refusal envelope exist. In apps/modn-cli/src/themes.rs (refuse), a refused modn theme register in --json mode prints {"error":"THEME_INVALID","message":message,"diagnostics":problems} on stderr and exits 1. The desktop (apps/modn-desktop/src/app/theme_diagnostics.rs) keeps diagnostics only for THEME_INVALID, caps at 50 entries with keys theme, path, line, rule and message, truncates message at 300 chars and theme at 130, strips control characters, and nulls absolute, .. or over-long paths.
