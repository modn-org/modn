Adoption requires both --acknowledge and --yes. Forced owned cleanup requires --force and --yes. Neither approval is inferred or prompted. Dry runs retain pending verification. Seven CLI journeys cover these distinctions. Ordinary uninstall remains strict. MCP and desktop expose neither adoption nor forced cleanup.
